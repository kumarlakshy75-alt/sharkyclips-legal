Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

SharkyClips is a locally operated application for creating short-form video clips.

Information and Data

When you connect your YouTube account, the application may access YouTube data and permissions authorized by you through Google OAuth.

Use of Information

Authorized information is used to support YouTube uploads and related features requested by the user.

Local Processing

Video processing is performed locally on your computer. Optional online services may be used when requested.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is not shared with third parties except where needed to provide requested services or comply with law.
